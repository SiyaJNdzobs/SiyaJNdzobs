<td><strong>IEC Local Government Elections Turnout Predictor Model</strong></td>
<td>

An empirical machine-learning forecasting and decision-support platform focused on predicting <strong>2026 ward-level voter turnout across KwaZulu-Natal</strong>.

The project combines 21 years of longitudinal IEC election records (2000–2021) with socioeconomic indicators, including Stats SA employment metrics and multidimensional poverty measures. It uses an ensemble <strong>Random Forest Regressor</strong> to investigate turnout patterns and generate predictive insights.

The platform includes interactive historical-period filtering, voter-to-population participation indicators, data visualisation, and spatial analysis.

KwaZulu-Natal is being used as a <strong>pilot province</strong> to evaluate the methodology, data pipeline, model performance, and supporting indicators before considering broader national scaling.

<br>

<strong>Reported model performance:</strong> MAE 4.59% · RMSE 6.31%
